The Trip

Five days on the Ligurian coast

Between aperitivos on the boat and the World Cup opener, five villages hewn into cliffsides, a hidden abbey reachable only by trail or sea, and the most photogenic fishing harbour in Italy — this is five days of salt air, good wine, and earned gelato.

02
Friday · 12 June
Cinque Terre
Five villages, cliffside trails, pesto & the sea 🏔️
▾
🚂
08:00 – 09:00
Train from Santa → Levanto
Levanto is the western terminus of the Cinque Terre Express — you pass through it anyway heading south. Journey takes 35 min to 1h15 depending on the service (€6.20–€9). From here the Treno MS Card (€29.50) covers everything to Riomaggiore and back.
⚠ Leave early — it's a Friday in June and the trains fill up fast.
🚂
CT Express → Riomaggiore
Take the Cinque Terre Express all the way east to Riomaggiore — the furthest village. You'll hike back westward from here.
🚶
Via dell'Amore: Riomaggiore → Manarola (20 min, 920m)
The legendary cliff-side walkway. Almost flat, paved, and jaw-dropping. Book your timed entry slot online in advance — included in the MS Card.
📍 Manarola is the most photogenic village — turquoise water, colourful houses stacked on the rock.
🚂
Train: Manarola → Corniglia
The Manarola–Corniglia trail is closed until 2029 due to landslides. Two-minute train, covered by card.
🥾
Hike: Corniglia → Vernazza (3.4 km, ~1h30)
Beautiful forested stretch with sea views. Corniglia is the only village with no harbour — perched on a cliff 100m above the sea. A 5-min bus from the station gets you up (included in card).
👟 Shoes: pretty chill overall — sneakers are fine.
🍽️
Lunch in Vernazza
Vernazza has the best harbour in the Cinque Terre — a tiny natural port ringed by coloured buildings. Find a terrace with trofie al pesto and local white wine.
🥾
Hike: Vernazza → Monterosso (4.2 km, ~2h, hardest stretch)
The most dramatic section of the Blue Trail — steep climbs, panoramic sea views. Finish in Monterosso, the only village with a proper sandy beach.
🏖️
Beach & swim in Monterosso
Earned. Relax, swim, gelato, cold drink. Then catch the CT Express back to Levanto and the regional train home to Santa.
03
Saturday · 13 June
San Fruttuoso & Portofino
Hidden abbey, boat day, group dinner ⛵
▾
🗺️
MORNING — SPLIT TRANSPORT
Choose your way to San Fruttuoso
San Fruttuoso is only reachable by sea or on foot — no roads. Split the group by preference and meet there for lunch.
🥾
~10:00
Option A — Hike from Portofino (~2h)
Walk the coastal trail from Portofino through Monte di Portofino natural park. Rocky and forested with sea views. Arrive San Fruttuoso around noon.
⛵
Option B — Boat from Santa
Samia arrives late morning — she takes the boat with whoever else wants it. Ferry runs from Santa Margherita to San Fruttuoso.
🍋
Lunch at San Fruttuoso
🏛️
Swim + visit the Abbazia di San Fruttuoso
10th-century Benedictine abbey on the water's edge. The Cristo degli Abissi bronze statue is submerged just offshore — visible snorkelling.
⛵
Boat or hike back to Portofino
🥂
~20:00
Evening in Portofino
Aperitivo, dinner at Yacht Club Portofino, maybe dancing.
